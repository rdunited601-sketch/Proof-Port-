## 🎯 **How to Use the Enhanced Metaverse Hub**

### **Step 1: Access the Application**
- **Main App**: `http://localhost:8000/index.html`
- **Feature Test**: `http://localhost:8000/test_features.html`
- **Verification**: `http://localhost:8000/verify.html`

### **Step 2: Create Your Ready Player Me Avatar**
1. **Click "Enter Room"** on any room card
2. **Select "Ready Player Me" tab**
3. **Choose Your Option**:
   - **🎨 Create Avatar**: Opens Ready Player Me creator
   - **👥 Browse Samples**: Select from 6 sample avatars
   - **📋 Paste URL**: Use your existing Ready Player Me avatar
4. **Click "🚀 Use This Avatar"**
5. **Enter the 3D Room**

### **Step 3: Experience Gaming Controls**
1. **Click on the 3D canvas** to enable mouse look
2. **WASD** to move around
3. **Mouse** to look around (like in games)
4. **Shift + WASD** to run
5. **Space** to jump
6. **Switch camera modes** using the Camera View buttons

### **Step 4: Interact with the Environment**
1. **Click on chairs** to sit down
2. **Click on table** to start meeting mode
3. **Click on whiteboard** for drawing mode
4. **Listen to character voices** in meetings
5. **Earn points** for interactions
