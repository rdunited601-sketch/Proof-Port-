## 🎯 **How to Use the Enhanced Metaverse Hub**

### **Step 1: Access the Application**
- Go to: `http://localhost:8000`
- Or use: `http://localhost:8000/index.html`

### **Step 2: Create Your Human-like Avatar**
1. Click "Enter Room" on any room card
2. Choose "Upload Photo" tab
3. Upload your photo
4. Select generation mode (Realistic/Stylized/Cartoon)
5. Customize hair, eyes, skin tone
6. Click "Generate Avatar Instantly"
7. Click "Enter Room"

### **Step 3: Experience Gaming Controls**
1. **Click on the 3D canvas** to enable mouse look
2. **WASD** to move around
3. **Mouse** to look around (like in games)
4. **Shift + WASD** to run
5. **Space** to jump
6. **Switch camera modes** using the Camera View buttons

### **Step 4: Interact with the Environment**
1. **Click on chairs** to sit down
2. **Click on table** to start meeting mode
3. **Click on whiteboard** for drawing mode
4. **Listen to character voices** in meetings
5. **Earn points** for interactions

### **Step 5: Explore Premium Features**
1. **Earn points** by interacting with objects
2. **Unlock premium avatars** as you gain points
3. **Customize avatars** with colors and effects
4. **Experience special effects** for premium avatars

### **Core Technologies**
- **Three.js**: 3D graphics and rendering
- **WebGL**: Hardware-accelerated graphics
- **WebRTC**: Real-time communication
- **Web Speech API**: Text-to-speech functionality
- **Pointer Lock API**: Gaming-style controls

### **Key Functions Implemented**
- `createHumanLikeAvatar()`: Human-like avatar creation
- `animateAvatarMovement()`: Walking and running animations
- `setupAudioOutput()`: Character voice system
- `updateCamera()`: Gaming-style camera controls
- `handleMovement()`: WASD movement with animations
- `playCharacterVoice()`: Text-to-speech for characters
